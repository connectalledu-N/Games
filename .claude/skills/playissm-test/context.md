## The umbrella: PLAYISSM

- **Standard description (use word for word on first mention):** PLAYISSM
  designs human-centred learning environments and sensory-informed spaces
  that combine neuroscience, design and play to improve learning, wellbeing
  and human experience.
- **Tagline:** Where play sparks possibility.
- **Sign-off:** Immersive. Inclusive. Mindful.
- **Why it exists:** people don't experience learning, play, sensation and
  emotion in separate boxes, so PLAYISSM designs them together.
- **Positioning:** where play, learning, sensory experience and human-centred
  design meet. "The space between disciplines": child development, education,
  play, sensory experience, environmental design and wellbeing, without being
  defined by any one of them.
- **Five threads:** Play, Learning, Sensory, Regulation, Human connection.
- **ISSM:** Immersive (people experience rather than receive), Self-Directed
  (choice, agency, individual pace), Synergetic (elements work together),
  Mindful (attention to the person, the environment and the moment).
- **Three guiding roots:** Rhythms, Modes, Invitations.
- **The Living Loop:** Rhythm (how people arrive) → Mode (how they engage) →
  Invitation (what is offered) → new rhythm.
- **We are:** human-centred, multidisciplinary, experience-led, thoughtful,
  contemporary, inclusive. Playful without being childish; sophisticated
  without being clinical.
- **We are not:** a traditional school, a therapy clinic, a conventional
  playground company, an interior design studio, a generic wellness brand,
  or a one-size-fits-all activity provider.
- **The umbrella owns** the belief, ISSM, Rhythms · Modes · Invitations, the
  Living Loop, brand identity, IP and research. It speaks to partners,
  institutions, designers, architects, developers and organisations.
- **Direction:** near term, prove the philosophy through real projects,
  experiences and physical environments. Longer term, build methodology, IP,
  learning spaces, programmes and partnerships that travel across
  geographies.
- **Contact:** connect@playissm.com · www.playissm.com

## The three verticals

Each vertical applies the philosophy to one audience and setting, with its own
offer and proof.

### PlaySense: sensory environments (works through environment)

- Sensory and regulation-led environments for public and shared spaces.
- Designs sensory environments, quiet spaces and regulation-led experiences.
  It shapes the whole experience: spatial planning, sensory cues, movement,
  sound, light, touch, pace, transitions and the needs of different users.
- Difference: more than a sensory room or a set of equipment; the
  environment itself is part of the experience, "purposeful, dignified and
  intuitive".
- For: airports and transport, hospitality, public spaces, real estate,
  workplaces, institutions, communities, organisations seeking inclusive
  environments.
- Accent colour: teal (assumed from the documents' order; confirm).

### Kagami 鏡: learning ecosystem (works through learning)

- A global studio school for rhythm-led, regulation-first learning.
- Studio-style learning, interdisciplinary exploration, projects, guided
  experiences and self-directed discovery, instead of a fixed sequence of
  instruction.
- Difference: not a conventional online school or a digital content library.
- For: children and families, learners outside traditional schooling,
  educators, schools and learning communities, collaborators.

### PlayStudio: physical experience (works through physical community experience)

- A physical ecosystem for play, learning, creativity and community.
- Activity spaces, creative studios, a reading and book area, a toy library,
  a café, and guided and self-directed experiences; people move between modes
  instead of separate programmes.
- Difference: more than a play café, activity centre or tuition space.
- Direction: a flagship physical model that becomes repeatable.
- For: children and families, communities, schools and educators, creators
  and partners.

## Brand rules (for website and brand reviews)

- Write PLAYISSM in capitals in text; "Playissm" appears only inside the logo.
- Verticals in CamelCase, singular, never abbreviated: PlaySense, Kagami,
  PlayStudio. First mention carries the descriptor.
- "Verticals" in business documents, "expressions" in brand storytelling.
- Colours: PLAYISSM logo lockup in Indigo, Deep Plum and Crimson; Crimson
  belongs to PLAYISSM alone. Verticals use Indigo, Ink, White and Brume plus
  one accent each (teal, yellow, orange). Accents fill shapes; text stays
  Indigo or Ink.
