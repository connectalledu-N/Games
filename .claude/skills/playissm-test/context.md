## Brand architecture

- **Playstudios**: umbrella venture; everything else rolls up here.
- **PLAYISSM**: parent brand; the ISSM methodology for designing learning
  experiences, and the main content platform.
  - **Kagami**: product line; learning-in-practice tooling under the PLAYISSM
    methodology ("a mirror for learning in practice").
  - **PlaySense**: product line; the neuroscience-of-play work. Its "Neuro
    Trilogy" has not shipped yet.

## Positioning so far

- Core claim: the way most learning is designed and measured today "skips a
  step that matters"; ISSM is the method that adds it.
- Tone: building in public, founder honesty, "no polish, no highlight reel".
- Content pillars: Build in Public, Kagami, Methodology, Founder Reality.
- Channels: LinkedIn (company and personal; a 4-post reveal arc), Substack (a
  5-essay launch arc), plus Instagram-style carousels and build videos.
- Team named in the plan: Neha (approves copy). Posts go out at 11:00 Paris
  time.
